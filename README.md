# 2. Problem Statement

## AI Learning Assistant & Personalized Study Planner

> **"Because no two students learn the same way — so why should they all get the same study plan?"**

Every student is different.

Some students understand concepts quickly and need advanced challenges, while others need more time and simpler explanations. However, traditional learning platforms often provide the same content, same pace and generic study schedules regardless of individual strengths, weaknesses and available time.

This creates several problems:

- Students spend time studying topics they already understand.
- Weak areas are often neglected.
- Exam preparation becomes last-minute cramming.
- Students may not receive immediate help when they are stuck.
- Personal tutoring can be expensive or inaccessible.
- Static study plans do not adapt when a student falls behind.

The official challenge asks for a full-stack web application where a student can define what they want to learn, receive a personalized study plan based on their knowledge and available time, and receive AI-powered help while studying.

# 3. Our Solution

## Introducing Adigam AI

**Adigam AI** is an adaptive learning platform designed to work like a personalized digital tutor and study planner.

Instead of giving every student the same learning path, Adigam AI continuously uses learning evidence to determine what the student should do next.
